# Interpret Uploaded ZIP Files As

This setting tells the app what kind of scene it should assume is inside uploaded ZIP or GZ archives.

## Why it matters

A compressed archive does not make its scene type obvious. The app needs to know whether the archive should be treated as:

- Cinema 4D
- Blender
- Octane Standalone

## Good to know

If you want to confirm the type each time instead of relying on a default, turn on the [ZIP confirmation](settings_show_confirm_zip.md) dialog.
