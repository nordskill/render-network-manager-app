# Usage Statistics Consent

> in development 

This setting controls whether the app may collect usage statistics.

## What that means

Usage statistics can help the team understand installation success, errors, and general app behavior so they can improve stability and usability.

## Your choice

You can accept or decline this from the consent banner, and the app respects that choice.

## Good to know

This is about app analytics, not your creative files or render output content.
