# Render Cost Calculator

The Render Cost Calculator helps you estimate how much a job will cost on the Render Network before you actually start rendering.

It works by comparing your local render time with the power of the Network. You tell the calculator how long [one frame renders](calculator_time_per_frame.md) on your machine, how many frames you need, and how fast your hardware is (your [OB Score](settings_ob_score.md)). From that, the app gives you a rough price estimate so you can plan your budget and choose the right render tier.

The estimate is not an exact price — final cost always depends on the actual render time on the Network — but it gives you a useful starting point so there are no big surprises.

The calculator is available only when you are signed in, because it uses the Render Network API.

If any of the estimated values appear in red, that means your account does not have enough Render Credits or RENDER Tokens to cover that tier.
