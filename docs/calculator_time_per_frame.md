# Time Per Frame

This field is where you enter how long one frame of your scene takes to render on your own machine.

The app uses this number together with your [OB Score](settings_ob_score.md) and frame count to estimate the total cost of a job. A realistic test gives you a better estimate.

## How to get a useful number

Render one frame, or a small range of frames, on your local machine. If your shot changes a lot over time, test a heavier frame or average a few frames instead of using the easiest one.

Choose the right unit in the calculator:

- seconds for short frames
- minutes for typical animation work
- hours for very heavy scenes

---
## Important

The estimate is only a guide. Final cost depends on the actual render time on the Network.
