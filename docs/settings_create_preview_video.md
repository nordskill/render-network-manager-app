# Create Preview Video

This option makes the app automatically build a preview video when you download preview frames for a job.

## Why artists use it

A quick MP4 is easier to review and share than a folder full of preview images, especially for animation checks.

## How it works

When you download previews, the app can turn the image sequence into a video automatically. You can still change this per job when working with preview downloads.

You can also customize the generated video by providing custom FFmpeg parameters in the [Preview Video Parameters](settings_video_params.md) setting. For example, you can overlay frame numbers on top of the video for easier review.

## Good to know

Single-frame jobs do not get a preview video because there is no sequence to turn into a clip.
