# OB Score

OB Score is the OctaneBench score used by the Render Cost Calculator to estimate how powerful your hardware is for rendering. 

## How it works

The calculator compares your computer's render speed with the Render Network. A more accurate OB Score gives you a more realistic price estimate.

Enter the OctaneBench score for the machine you used to test your scene. If you already saved your OB Score in Settings, the calculator can fill it in for you automatically.

---
## Good to know

This number is only used for estimating cost before you render. It does not change the quality of your job, the speed tier you choose, or the actual rendering hardware used by the Render Network.

---

You can measure your own score by running [OctaneBench](https://render.otoy.com/octanebench/) — it is free and does not require an OctaneRender license. If you do not have OctaneBench installed, you can look up a similar GPU configuration in the [OctaneBench results table](https://render.otoy.com/octanebench/results.php) and use that number as an approximation.

## When to update it

Change it if you benchmark a different machine, upgrade your hardware, or want to use a more accurate score for cost estimates. Saving it in Settings means you do not need to type the same value into the calculator every time.
