# Confirm ZIP Type Before Upload

This option decides whether the app asks you what kind of scene is inside a ZIP or GZ archive before upload.

- **When enabled:**  
  The app shows a confirmation dialog so you can choose the scene type for each upload batch.
- **When disabled:**  
  The app skips the confirmation and uses your saved [ZIP interpretation](settings_interpret_zip.md) setting automatically.

Leaving confirmation on is safer if you upload different scene types. Turning it off is faster if your archive workflow is always the same.
