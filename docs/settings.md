# App Settings

The Settings panel is where you customize how the Render Network Manager behaves on your machine. Most defaults work well out of the box, so you only need to change things when your workflow requires.

Settings cover areas like where downloads and temporary files are stored, how uploads are prepared and compressed, how the app handles archives and preview videos, and how aggressively it downloads frames. There are also options for storing your [OctaneBench score](settings_ob_score.md) for the cost calculator and fine-tuning advanced features like [Differential Upload](settings_differential_upload.md) and chunk sizes.

If you are just getting started, the defaults are a safe choice. As you get more comfortable with the app, you can come back here and adjust things to match the way you work.
