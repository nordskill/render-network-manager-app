# Render Job Details

The job details view is where you set up and review everything about a render job before it goes to the Network and where you manage it once it comes back.

When creating a job, you pick which render targets / takes to render, choose a preset or an earlier job as your starting point, review render settings like resolution and frame range, select the output formats and passes you need, and decide on cost-related options such as render tier and GPU limits. You also choose where the finished frames will be saved on your computer.

Once the job is rendering, you can track its progress, and when frames are ready you can download exactly the outputs and passes you want.
