# Job Download Location

This section controls where the finished files for a job will be saved on your computer.

You can keep the app's default download location or choose a different folder for a specific job or preset.

## Good to know

Other download settings affect the final folded folder structure:

- **[Create job folder](settings_create_job_folder.md)** can place each job inside its own folder
- **[Smart folders](settings_smart_folders.md)** can sort files by output and pass
- **[Save next to scene file](job_download_save_to_scene.md)** overrides the custom location and keeps everything beside your source scene
