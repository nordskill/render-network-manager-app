# Instant Download Manager

The Instant Download Manager is the quickest way to get your rendered frames from the Render Network onto your computer. It works right from the app's home screen — no sign-in needed.

If you already have direct download links from the Render Network website, you can paste them from the clipboard, drop a text or CSV file with links into the app, or use a supported website download button that hands the links over automatically. The app takes care of the rest and saves the files to your [chosen folder](instant_download_location.md).

This is especially handy when you just want to grab your finished frames without loading your full scene and job list into the Manager first.
